### How to add activity in annual calendar?

Thu, 05 Sep, 2024

[Articles](https://smart-hospital.in/articles)[Annual Calendar](https://smart-hospital.in/category/annual-calendar)

---

To add an activity to the annual calendar, go to the Annual Calendar module in your Smart Hospital system and click on it. The Annual Calendar page will open on the right side.

![](../assets/how-to-add-activity-in-annual-calendar/1.jpg)  
  
Now go to the **Add** button, showing at the top right corner and click on it, Add window will be open In this window, you can see options for Type: **Holiday**, **Activity**, and **Vacation**. Simply select **'Activity'** by clicking on it, then choose the date when the activity will occur. Add a description of the activity , and if you want to display this activity with details on the front site of your Smart Hospital, enable the **'Front Site'** button.   
  
![](../assets/how-to-add-activity-in-annual-calendar/2.jpg)  
  
Finally, click the **'Save'** button to save your activity details in the holiday list.  
  
![](../assets/how-to-add-activity-in-annual-calendar/3.jpg)  
  
To **edit** the activity details simply go to the pencil icon showing at the right side and click on it, edit activity window will be open  
  
![](../assets/how-to-add-activity-in-annual-calendar/4.jpg)  
  
Now update the details you want to change, then click the **'Save'** button. Your activity details will be updated successfully.

To **delete** the activity details, simply click on the delete icon on the right side, then click the **'OK'** button. The activity details will be deleted successfully  
  
![](../assets/how-to-add-activity-in-annual-calendar/5.jpg)  
  
If you want to search specifically for activity details, go to the top left of the page. Here, you'll see a **'Type'** dropdown menu. Select the **'Activity'** option from the dropdown  
  
![](../assets/how-to-add-activity-in-annual-calendar/6.jpg)  
  
Then click on **Search**, in below list only display the activity details.  
  
![](../assets/how-to-add-activity-in-annual-calendar/7.jpg)  
  
  
Additionally you can also see the activity details on the front site of your Smart Hospital, as you enabled this option when adding the activity. Simply go to the front site of your Smart Hospital and go to the **annual calendar**  
  
![](../assets/how-to-add-activity-in-annual-calendar/8.jpg)  
  
Here you can see the activity details which you recently added from the annual calendar

Now, You can see the activity details on the **calendar** in both the **superadmin** and **patient** logins.  
  
**For Superadmin login**, you can see a calendar icon at the top right corner. Click on it to open the calendar, where you can view the activity details for the specific day you added

![](../assets/how-to-add-activity-in-annual-calendar/9.jpg)

**For patient login**, you can see a calendar icon at the top right corner. Click on it to open the calendar, where you can view the activity details for the specific day you added  
  
![](../assets/how-to-add-activity-in-annual-calendar/10.jpg)
