### About Front CMS

Thu, 11 Nov, 2021

[Articles](https://smart-hospital.in/articles)[Front CMS](https://smart-hospital.in/category/front-cms)

---

In Smart Hospital, we have provided a option to design your Hospital front site page. To design your hospital front page go to Front CMS, here you can add or edit **Pages**, upload images and videos at **Media Manager** which can be used for  Smart Hospital, add **Menus** like **Main Menu** and **Bottom Menu** and **Banner** Images.  
  
For Front CMS setup please [click here](https://smart-hospital.in/article/front-cms-setting) to see **Front CMS Setting**.
