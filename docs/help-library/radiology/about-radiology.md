### About Radiology

Thu, 11 Nov, 2021

[Articles](https://smart-hospital.in/articles)[Radiology](https://smart-hospital.in/category/radiology)

---

Radiology is also known as **diagnostic imaging**. Radiology is useful in several operations such as CT scan, MRI, X-ray, ultrasound, etc required during diagnosis. Radiology is the principle management process of all the disease. It also contains a variety of tools and techniques for detection, analyzing, and curing.  
  
In Smart Hospital we have provided the feature so staff can arrange patient's radiology tests, payments for radiology  tests and provide radiology test reports through Smart Hospital application to patients.
