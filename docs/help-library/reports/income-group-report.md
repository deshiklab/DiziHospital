Sat, 30 Oct, 2021

[Articles](https://smart-hospital.in/articles)[Reports](https://smart-hospital.in/category/reports)

---

To check Income Group report, go to **Reports > Income** **Group** **Report**. Here select the **Time Duration** **and Search Income Head** then click on**Search** button. Here you can see Income Group details like **Income Head**, **Income Id, Name, Date, Invoice Number, Amount** etc. Also you can download this report as PDF.
