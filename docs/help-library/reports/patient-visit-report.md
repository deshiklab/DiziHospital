Sat, 30 Oct, 2021

[Articles](https://smart-hospital.in/articles)[Reports](https://smart-hospital.in/category/reports)

---

To check Patient Visit Report, go to **Reports > Patient Visit Report**. Here enter the Patient Id (you will get patient id from OPD or IPD) then click on**Search** button. Here you can see **Patient Visit** details like **OPD, IPD, Pathology, Radiology** etc. Also you can download this report as PDF.
