### Expense Group Report

Sat, 30 Oct, 2021

[Articles](https://smart-hospital.in/articles)[Reports](https://smart-hospital.in/category/reports)

---

To check Expense Group report, go to **Reports > Expense** **Group** **Report**. Here select the **Time Duration** **and Search Expense Head** then click on**Search** button. Here you can see Expense Group details like Expense Head, Expense Id, Name, Date, Invoice Number, Amount etc. Also you can download this report as PDF.
