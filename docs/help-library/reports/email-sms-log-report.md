### Email / SMS Log Report

Sat, 30 Oct, 2021

[Articles](https://smart-hospital.in/articles)[Reports](https://smart-hospital.in/category/reports)

---

To check Email/SMS Log report, go to **Reports >** **Email / SMS Log** **Report**. Here select the **Time Duration** then click on**Search** button. Here you can see **Email/SMS Log** details like **Title, Date, Email, SMS, Group, Individual** etc. Also you can download this report as PDF.
