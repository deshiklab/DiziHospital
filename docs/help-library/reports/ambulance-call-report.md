### Ambulance Call Report.

Fri, 29 Oct, 2021

[Articles](https://smart-hospital.in/articles)[Reports](https://smart-hospital.in/category/reports)

---

To check Ambulance Call report, go to **Reports >** **Ambulance Call** **Report**. Here select the **Time Duration**, **Collected By and Vehicle Model** then click on**Search** button. Here you can see Ambulance Call details like **Ambulance Call No, Patient Name, Date, Contact Number, Vehicle Number, Vehicle Model, Driver Name, Address, Ambulance, Amount** etc. Also you can download this report as PDF.
