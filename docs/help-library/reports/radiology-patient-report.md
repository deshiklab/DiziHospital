### Radiology Patient Report

Sat, 30 Oct, 2021

[Articles](https://smart-hospital.in/articles)[Reports](https://smart-hospital.in/category/reports)

---

To check Radiology Patient report, go to **Reports >** **Radiology Patient** **Report**. Here select the **Time Duration, Sample Collected Person Name Category Name,** **and** **Test Name** then click on**Search** button. Here you can see patient radiology details like **Bill No, Patient Name, Category Name, Test Name, Consultant Doctor, Sample Collected Person Name, Amount, Paid Amount, Balance Amount** **etc**. Also you can download this report as PDF.
