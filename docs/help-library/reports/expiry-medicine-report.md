### Expiry Medicine Report

Sat, 30 Oct, 2021

[Articles](https://smart-hospital.in/articles)[Reports](https://smart-hospital.in/category/reports)

---

To check Expiry Medicine report, go to **Reports > Expiry Medicine Report**. Here select **Time Duration**, **Medicine Category, Supplier** then click on **Search** button. Here you can see Expiry Medicine Report details like **Medicine Name, Batch No, Company Name, Medicine Category, Medicine Group, Supplier, Expire Date** and **Qty (Quantity).** Also you can download this report as PDF.
