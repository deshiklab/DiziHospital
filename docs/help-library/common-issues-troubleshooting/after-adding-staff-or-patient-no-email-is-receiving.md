### After adding staff or patient no email is receiving?

Mon, 18 Oct, 2021

[Articles](https://smart-hospital.in/articles)[Common Issues & Troubleshooting](https://smart-hospital.in/category/common-issues-troubleshooting)

---

After **adding staff or patient if no email is receiving** or **there is no any email send by system** then your email setting is not configured properly so system is unable to send any email. To configure email please check [this article](https://smart-hospital.in/article/email-send-not-working "Email Send Not Working").
