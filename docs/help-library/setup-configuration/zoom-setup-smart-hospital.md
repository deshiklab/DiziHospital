### Zoom Setup - Smart Hospital

Thu, 21 Nov, 2024

[Articles](https://smart-hospital.in/articles)[Setup & Configuration](https://smart-hospital.in/category/setup-configuration)

---

To configure **Zoom Live****Consultation**, go to **Setup > Zoom Setting**, On the right side setting page will be open.  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/1.jpg)  
  
On this page, you can see multiple configuration options are available. To generate zoom api credential, simply click on the **'click here'** link, which is shown in blue on the right above.  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/2.jpg)  
  
After click on the click here link, Zoom App Marketplace page will open.  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/3.jpg)

To sign in for Zoom, simply click on the **'Sign In'** button, which is available at the top right corner. After click, **sign in** page will be open.  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/4.jpg)  
  
Now, fill in your details and click on the **'Sign In'** button to log in to the Zoom application. After successfully login in zoom application, page will be open.  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/5.jpg)

In this page you can see the multiple option, now click on **Develop** dropdown, showing at the top right corner, when you click on develop button drop down list will be open, now here you can see the **Build App** option available.  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/6.jpg)

Simply click on **Build App** option and after click on it you have get option to choose what kind of app you want to create   
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/7.jpg)  
  
Please select **General App** then click on **Create** button  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/8.jpg)  
  
After clicked on create button, you need to enter the Basic Information  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/9.jpg)  
Now click on the **Continue** button at the bottom right so it will redirect to the **Access** page then click on continue button   
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/10.jpg)  
  
Here **Surface** page will be open and select the **Meetings** and click on **Continue** button   
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/11.jpg)  
After click on continue Embed page will be open   
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/12.jpg)  
   
Now enabled the **Meeting SDK** and the page will open in below  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/13.jpg)  
then click on Continue button, Scope page will be open, here click on **Add Scope** button  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/14.jpg)  
  
After clicking on **Add Scope**, an Add Scope window will open. First, ensure that **User > View user's zak token** is selected by default.  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/15.jpg)  
  
then click on **Meeting**, in meeting here you can see the options, kindly follow the image option and select the all check box's under of them.  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/16.jpg)  
  
then ensure zoom app should also be checked in below   
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/17.jpg)  
  
now click on done all Scope will be added successfully.  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/18.jpg)  
Now click on the **Continue** button. The App Listing page will open. On this page, enter the relevant details as desired.  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/19.jpg)  
then click on continue button **Link & Support** page will be open, here enter your URL's depending upon your requirement  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/20.jpg)  
  
Now click on the **Continue** button again. The Technical Design page will open. On this page, first update the document in the architecture diagram section, then provide the relevant answers for the application development.   
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/21.jpg)  
After that, click on the **Continue** button then give the relevant answer for the security.  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/22.jpg)  
  
then again click on continue privacy page will be open, here give the relevant answer for the privacy.  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/23.jpg)  
now click on Continue button Beta Test page will be open from here you can regenerate the Authorization URL if you made any changes in previous settings.  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/24.jpg)  
now click on continue button App Submission page will be open here follow the instructions and then click on Submit button  
  
![](../assets/zoom-setup-smart-hospital/25.jpg)  
After click on submit button you have successfully generated zoom api credentials
