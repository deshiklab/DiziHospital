### How to add Department?

Tue, 26 Oct, 2021

[Articles](https://smart-hospital.in/articles)[Human Resource](https://smart-hospital.in/category/human-resource)

---

To add department, go to **Setup** > **Human Resource > Department** then enter **Name** and then click on**Save** button.

You can view this added record in the **Department List** on the right side of the page.

To edit department click on**Edit** icon and to delete department click on **Delete** icon present in the Department list.
