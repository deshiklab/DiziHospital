### How to add new Medicine?

Mon, 10 Jun, 2024

[Articles](https://smart-hospital.in/articles)[Pharmacy](https://smart-hospital.in/category/pharmacy)

---

To add new medicine click on **+ Add Medicine** button here enter **Medicine Name, Medicine Category, Medicine Company, Medicine Composition, Medicine Group, Supplier, Unit, Minimum Level, Reorder Level, Tax, Box/Packing, VAT Account, Rack Number, Note** and **Medicine Photo.**![](../assets/how-to-add-new-medicine/1.jpg)  
  
To see medicine details and stock entry click on **Show** button, here you can also check medicine stock entry and bad stock entry details.
