### How to Setup 2FA?

Sat, 16 Aug, 2025

[Articles](https://smart-hospital.in/articles)[Two Factor Authentication](https://smart-hospital.in/category/two-factor-authentication)

---

Go to the your **Smart Hospital** to configure 2FA of Two Factor Authentication, two-step verification. For **setup 2FA**, go to the **Two Factor Authentication** module, which is shown in the left side bar. Now click on**Setup 2FA,** the **Setup 2FA** page will open.  
  
![](../assets/how-to-setup-2fa/1.jpg)

**Note:** For further processing, you need to set up **Google Authenticator** on your mobile device.
