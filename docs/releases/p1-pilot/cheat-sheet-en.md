# Reception and appointments — quick guide

**DRAFT · TRAINING ONLY · NOT APPROVED FOR LIVE PATIENT CARE**<br>
**Proposed audience:** reception / appointment desk · **Branch, scope, version:** complete from the approved pilot notice before printing.

## 1. Before you start

- Read the current, approved branch/module ownership notice. Use the new platform only for a module marked `N` for this branch. `L` means use the authorized legacy path; `P`, blank, or unclear means stop and ask your supervisor. **Never enter the same transaction in both systems.**
- Sign in with your own account. Confirm the branch and role you were assigned; do not share accounts.
- Verify the patient using the hospital-approved identity-check process before creating or changing a record.

## 2. Register or book

- Work only in the system that currently owns the module.
- Check the patient, requested date/time, and clinician details before confirming the task.
- Wait for a clear completion result. If the result is missing or uncertain, check the record/status with your supervisor before trying again; do not create a second booking just in case.

## 3. Offline, pending, or unexpected status

- Do not assume an offline or pending item has reached the other system. Do not repeatedly submit it, clear a queue, or switch systems yourself.
- Follow the site's approved downtime procedure. Tell the shift supervisor and IT/support through the approved private channel. Record only a safe system reference and time in the support ticket—never patient details.

## 4. Stop and escalate

Stop the affected task and contact the clinician and supervisor immediately for a wrong-patient concern, possible duplicate, clinical safety issue, missing/incorrect record, unexplained conflict, or suspected data loss. Do not delete or “fix” a record to make the screen look right. Follow the site's emergency-care procedure if care is at risk.

## 5. Rollback is not a desk action

Staff do not change ownership, switch back to legacy, or copy entries between systems. The designated clinical owner and incident lead decide; authorized operations/IT staff carry out the change and reconciliation. Keep working only when the supervisor confirms the approved route.
